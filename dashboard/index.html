How the radar works

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth. The category multiple is the median market-cap/TVL ratio of the live-token protocols of the same category inside this snapshot (at least 3 comparables, otherwise the snapshot-wide median), clamped to 0.02–1.50. Growth is the 7-day TVL change clamped to −50…+100%, weighted 0.40. Maturity runs from 0.70 for a protocol tracked less than a day to 1.00 after four years with no token; a protocol with no tracking date scores 0.85. Breadth adds 0.04 per extra chain, up to 5. No protocol name is ever special-cased: the same formula scores every row.

What “pre-token” means. A protocol is on this board when its DefiLlama record carries no token — no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol. That is what the record says, never proof it has no token: a protocol on the list may still have a token its record does not carry. The watchlist is the house's floor (TVL at or above $50,000,000, no token, not a wrapped, staked or bridged product); the board also ranks every other rankable pre-token protocol below that floor, because the radar exists to find the early ones.

Farm plans. Each row carries six steps with what they cost and how long they take, derived from the protocol's own category, chains, TVL and age. Nothing here predicts whether or when a token is issued — the board prices the launch if the record stayed as it is.
